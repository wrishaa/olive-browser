Welcome to Olive

Olive keeps your browsing on this Mac. A few optional steps and you’re set.

Stays on this Mac
Tabs, history, imports and site choices are stored locally. No Olive account or cloud.
Ads are blocked by default
uBO Lite basic network blocking is on. Cosmetic filtering and per-site modes aren’t supported yet; turn blocking off from the shield button.
Permissions only when you need them
Olive asks macOS for the microphone, camera or screen only when you choose to set them up.

Sites and your search engine still see what you send them.

Calls

Sites like Meet and Zoom can use your microphone and camera once macOS allows Olive to.

Microphone
Camera

Next, macOS asks about your microphone, then your camera. Each site still asks you separately.

Screen Sharing

Share a window or your whole screen in calls. You choose what to share each time a site asks.

Screen & System Audio Recording
Not allowed

Next, macOS opens System Settings. Turn on Olive under Screen & System Audio Recording, then come back here. Olive has to quit and reopen once.

Import from Another Browser

Copy signed-in sessions so you can start where you left off.

Copies cookies and signed-in sessions only, not passwords, history or bookmarks. macOS may ask for your login password to unlock the browser’s Keychain item; Olive uses it on this Mac only. Your other browser isn’t changed. Existing Olive cookies are kept. Some sites will still ask you to sign in again.
